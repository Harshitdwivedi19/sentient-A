Sentiment Trajectory Analysis Guide

1. What does this application do?

Sentiment analysis: Predicts whether an individual message expresses positive, neutral, or negative sentiment.

Trajectory analysis: Models how those sentiment predictions evolve over time across sequential messages in a conversation.

2. Key Concepts & Formulas

• Polarity Score (s_t): Mathematical score defined as s_t = P(positive) - P(negative) in range [-1.0, +1.0].

• Context Window (k): Preceding conversational turns prepended to the target utterance to give the Transformer conversational memory.

• Candidate Turning Point: An inflection point where turn-to-turn score change |Δs| >= τ or where a sign reversal across neutral occurs.

• Volatility (σ): Standard deviation of score changes (Δs) across the conversation.

3. Scientific Accuracy & Limitations

• Polarity scores are model-derived indicators, NOT direct measurements of human internal emotions.

• Sarcasm, cultural idioms, and non-verbal cues may affect model classification.

• An identified turning point is an algorithmic flag, not proof of real-world interpersonal conflict or intent.
