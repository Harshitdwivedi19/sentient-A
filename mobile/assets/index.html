User Guide & Help Manual

Everyday explanations of how to import chats, read charts, and understand what the analysis can and cannot tell you.

1. How to Export & Import a WhatsApp Chat

Open WhatsApp on your phone, open any chat, tap the three dots in the top right -> More -> Export Chat -> choose Without Media. Save the resulting .txt file and tap + Import at the top of this app.

2. What Happens During Analysis?

Your chat is parsed line-by-line into sequential dialogue turns in your device's temporary memory (RAM). The context-aware sentiment model evaluates each message along with its preceding context to assess whether the wording expresses positive appreciation, neutral information, or critical dissatisfaction.

3. What Does Tone Mean in this App?

Every message receives a polarity score between -1.0 (negative) and +1.0 (positive), with 0.0 representing neutral:

  • Positive (+0.15 to +1.0): Gratitude, agreement, relief, helpfulness.
  • Neutral (-0.15 to +0.15): Factual inquiries, logistics, routine status updates.
  • Negative (-1.0 to -0.15): Frustration, dissatisfaction, criticism, urgency.

4. Important Limitations: What the AI Cannot Tell You

This app evaluates linguistic text only. It does NOT diagnose psychological health, evaluate interpersonal relationships, or determine what someone was truly feeling internally. Sarcasm, irony, in-jokes, and cultural idioms can easily be misclassified. Always review the actual message text in the Messages tab.

5. How to Trace Results to Supporting Messages

Every finding on the Overview and every point on the Timeline is directly linked to the conversation text. Tap any notable moment or message bubble to view the message alongside its 3 preceding context turns.

6. Privacy & 100% On-Device Processing

Your conversation messages are analyzed strictly inside your device's local memory (RAM). No chat text is sent to cloud servers or stored in remote databases. When you close the app or tap Purge Data in Settings, all data is immediately discarded.
