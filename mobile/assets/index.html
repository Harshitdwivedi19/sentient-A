How Sentient Trajectory Works

1. What is this app?

Sentient Trajectory helps you understand conversations better by reading the emotional tone of text messages and showing how the mood evolved over time.

2. The 3 Easy Charts Explained

• 🌊 Emotional Journey Arc: A continuous line showing whether the chat had a happy start, a tense conflict, and a satisfying recovery.

• 🗺️ Topic Map: Groups messages into topics (like Technical Issues, Customer Support, or Resolutions) and shows what people discussed most.

• 🔀 Mood Flow: Shows how feelings evolved from one message to the next (e.g. how many neutral or frustrated questions turned positive).

3. Privacy & Ethics

• 100% On-Device: Your messages never leave your phone.

• Helpful Guide, Not a Diagnosis: This app observes language tone to give you helpful conversational insights.
