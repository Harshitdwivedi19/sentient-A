Privacy & Data Ethics Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026 • Effective Date: October 25, 2026

Sentient Trajectory ("the Application", "we", "our") is designed as an explainable conversational sentiment intelligence tool. We believe personal messages and communications are strictly confidential. This Policy sets forth our global compliance with international cyber laws, user data rights, responsible AI ethics, and transparent ad monetization.

Zero-Data-Leakage Guarantee: All natural language processing, sentiment polarity calculations, and WhatsApp chat evaluations execute 100% locally on your device. No messages, speaker names, or analysis results are ever uploaded to any external server or shared with advertisers.

1. International Legal Compliance Framework

This Policy complies with the highest international cyber regulations:

  • GDPR (EU 2016/679): European Union General Data Protection Regulation (Arts. 12–23).
  • CCPA / CPRA: California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by California Privacy Rights Act.
  • DPDP Act 2023: Digital Personal Data Protection Act (India).
  • COPPA: Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506).
  • Google Play Store Developer Program & Families Policy.

2. 100% Local On-Device Processing

All conversation parsing and AI sentiment scoring run inside volatile device RAM:

  • No Remote Storage: The Application operates independently without storing, transmitting, or backing up conversation text or speaker tags to any cloud server.
  • Ephemeral Processing: Closing the app or clearing the conversation immediately purges active dialogue text from device RAM.

3. Advertising & Monetization Disclosures

To provide Sentient Trajectory as a free service, we display non-intrusive advertisements (such as Google AdMob):

A. Privacy Firewall

Advertisers and ad networks NEVER receive conversation text, chat logs, speaker identities, sentiment scores, or turning points. There is a complete separation between our local AI engine and third-party ad networks.

B. Standard Ad Network Signals

Ad partners may collect standard, non-identifying technical signals solely for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and performance metrics:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device model, OS version, and network configuration
  • Coarse geographic location (country/city level via IP address)

C. User Ad Choices & Opt-Out

  • Android Settings: Navigate to Settings > Google > Ads to toggle "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Reset Advertising ID".
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): We confirm that we do not sell or share user personal data or chat text.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & DPDP Act Compliance)

Sentient Trajectory is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older. In compliance with COPPA and Section 9 of the India DPDP Act 2023, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, nor do we permit behavioral profiling or targeted advertising directed at minors.

6. Responsible AI & Cyber Ethics

We adhere to strict Responsible AI and cyber security practices:

  • Non-Causal Indicators: Model-predicted sentiment polarities reflect probabilistic linguistic patterns, not psychiatric medical diagnoses.
  • Formula-Injection Defense: Exported CSV files sanitize formula triggers (=, +, -, @) to prevent spreadsheet code injection vulnerabilities.
