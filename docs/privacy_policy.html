Privacy Policy for Sentient Trajectory

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

Sentient Trajectory ("the Application", "we", "our") is designed as an explainable, research-grade natural language processing tool for analyzing conversation sentiment trajectories. We believe conversational data is strictly personal. This Privacy Policy outlines our comprehensive commitment to user privacy and data security.

1. 100% On-Device Local Processing

All natural language processing, sentiment polarity calculations, context-window aggregation, and turning-point analyses execute locally on your device. The Application operates independently without transmitting conversation text, messages, speaker identities, timestamps, or analysis results to any remote server or third-party cloud service.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, monetize, or transmit:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, or email addresses.
  • Contents of your pasted conversation text or imported WhatsApp .txt chat logs.
  • Calculated sentiment scores, turning-point locations, or generated narrative reports.
  • Device identifiers, geolocation data, contacts, or browsing history.

3. File Access & WhatsApp Chat Import

When you choose to analyze an exported WhatsApp chat file or text document, the file is read in volatile device memory (RAM) solely for the duration of turn parsing and scoring. No files are uploaded externally, and no other files or directories on your device are read or modified.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

The Application contains no advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking frameworks, and no telemetry software. We do not profile users or monitor user activity.

5. Defensive Data Export (Formula-Injection Defenses)

When you export analytical records to CSV, the Application incorporates defensive sanitization (Risk #87) by prepending dangerous formula prefixes (=, +, -, @) with an apostrophe (') to prevent spreadsheet code injection vulnerabilities.

6. Contact

For questions regarding this policy or the mathematical methodology of the application, please consult our public project repository.
